# SO-101 pick-and-place: progress summary

Status as of 22 Oct 2026. Task: pick up the eraser and place it in the tray, in MuJoCo sim, to build a demonstration-to-policy pipeline for SmolVLA.

| # | What was done | Results | What was learned |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scene + scripted policy**: SO-101 in MuJoCo with table, eraser, tray, wrist camera and external camera. IK solver, success test, scripted pick-and-place (`verify_pick_place.py`). | The scripted policy solves the default pose. | It uses a fixed grasp roll and ignores eraser rotation, so it can't produce demos for rotated erasers. |
| 2 | **Workspace measurement**: swept the IK over the tabletop. | Reachable region for a top-down grasp: x 0.14–0.26 m, y −0.04–0.16 m, yaw ±90°. | Outside it `wrist_flex` hits its ±95° limit and the grasp misses by about 40 mm. Because the eraser is 180°-symmetric, ±90° covers every rotation. |
| 3 | **Manual teleop demos**: keyboard teleop with recording at 33 Hz. Saves actions plus the full MuJoCo state; camera images are re-rendered from the state later. | 11 demos, all successful, all replaying bit-exactly from actions alone. | Saving the full state rather than images keeps files at about 0.7 MB each. `teleop_009` contains a regrasp. |
| 4 | **MimicGen-style augmentation**, our own implementation (`mimicgen.py`) of the idea from the MimicGen paper, not the MimicGen library. Each demo is split at the gripper events, then the reach-and-grasp part is moved to a new eraser pose on the table plane (position and rotation only, not full 3D). 10 demos used as sources in turn (`teleop_009` excluded). | 165 generated episodes from about 81% of attempts. | The flat-only transform works only because all grasps are straight down. It needed three fixes: a lead-in bridge, IK solved to convergence for each waypoint, and wrist roll corrected for shoulder pan (15% → 60% → 81%). |
| 5 | **Tail trimming** (`trim_demo.py`): each episode is cut 0.3 s after the success test first holds. | Removed tails averaging 1.1 s (teleop) and 10.9 s (generated); about 60k frames dropped in total. | MimicGen copies the whole source tail and its tracker is slow, so generated episodes were about 40% idle. |
| 6 | **LeRobot export** (`export_lerobot.py`) on Iridis, rendering through EGL on GPU nodes. | 176 episodes, 124,191 frames, 2 cameras at 640×480, in LeRobot v3 format. Took 1 h 47 min. | EGL rendering works on Iridis GPU nodes, so the sim can render and run evals there. |
| 7 | **ACT baseline training** on Iridis L4: 40k steps, batch 8, 100-step action chunks. | Completed in 2 h 29 min; final loss about 0.09 (about 2.5 epochs). | Learning rate fixed at 1e-5 with no schedule. |
| 8 | **Evaluation** (`eval_policy.py`): closed-loop rollouts in the live sim on 50 eraser poses not used in training. Pipeline checks: eval renders match the training frames, and offline predictions match the recorded actions. | **42%** (21/50) on new poses, 60% (12/20) on training poses. Checkpoints 10k/20k/30k/40k: 10% / 20% / 40% / 40% on the same 20 poses. | The model fails even on poses it trained on, so the main problem is grasping reliability, not generalisation. 26 of 29 failures never picked the eraser up. Weak below y < 0.06 (18% vs 61%). Success stopped improving between 30k and 40k. |
| 9 | **Re-planning test**: re-plan more often at run time (temporal ensembling; plans of 25 and 10 steps). | 20% / 2% / 0%, all worse than 42%. | The arm hovers above the eraser indefinitely. 57–65% of pre-grasp frames in the demos have the arm held still (teleop pauses, copied by MimicGen), so the policy learned that "hold still" is the most likely next action. |
| 10 | **v2 Stage 1: scripted expert for any eraser pose** (`pick_place_task.py`, see `plans/baseline-v2.md`). Wrist roll matched to eraser yaw at hover; grasp 1 cm lower; lower approach height near the base; carry routed around the base; dry-run choice between the two equivalent wrist rolls; stall detection instead of 21 s creeping. `verify_pick_place.py -n` measures its success rate; `record_scripted_demo.py --randomize` records. | **98%** (195/200) on fresh poses, so it passes the 95% gate. 200 demos recorded (`demos/scripted_trimmed`), all replay bit-exactly. Episodes 11.4 s vs ~20 s; arm still before the grasp **13%** of frames vs 57–65%. | The old script only worked near its tuned pose. The grasp was marginal (pinched the top of the eraser), and the site's offset from the roll axis makes one of the two equivalent rolls unreachable near the base. Remaining failures are in the corner between base and tray. |
| 11 | **v2 Stages 0 and 2: faster, fairer loop.** Fixed 100-pose test set (`eval/test_poses.json`), sharded eval + `merge_eval.py` (3 min for 100 poses), 320×240 sharded export (`iridis/*.sh`), throughput test. | v1 ACT on the 100 poses: **35%** (CI 26–45%), rerun 34%. Scripted demos exported to `scripted_320` (200 episodes, 76k frames) in about 12 min. ACT trains at 147 samples/s at batch 32 (4× v1). | The 42% was partly luck: the second half of the set gives 28%. Evaluation is not deterministic (15/100 pose outcomes flip between identical runs), so close comparisons need repeats. Batch 32, about 48k steps ≈ 20 epochs ≈ 3 h. |
| 12 | **v2 Stage 3a: single-pose overfit test.** 50 scripted demos at one pose, ACT 8000 steps at batch 32. | lr 1e-5 (default, as in v1): 0/20 at the centre pose, 11/20 on training poses. **lr 1e-4: 20/20 and 20/20**, gate passed. | The pipeline is sound. A trace showed the low-lr policy 8 mm off in the descent, nudging the eraser away: v1's learning rate was too low. Full run on 200 demos now uses lr 1e-4. |
| 13 | **v2 Stage 3b: ACT on 200 scripted demos** (320×240, batch 32, lr 1e-4, 48k steps ≈ 20 epochs, 3 h). | **78%** on the fixed 100 poses (CI 69–85%), 80% at the 28.8k checkpoint, against v1's 35%. Re-planning every 25/50 steps or temporal ensembling: 80/77/72%, no gain. | All failures are grasp misses: the jaw pushes the eraser during the descent, mostly on the far side (y > 0.09). Remaining gap is precision; next try recovery (noise-injected) demos and/or more demos. |

## Where we are

ACT baseline: **42% on new poses**, below the 70% bar in `plans/train-pick-place-policy.md`. The bottleneck is the training data, not the model or the evaluation.

## Next

Following `plans/baseline-v2.md`: Stages 0, 1 and 2 are done. Stage 3 baseline reached: **78–80%** on the fixed 100 poses (v1: 35%). Checkpoint: `outputs/train/act_scripted_lr1e4/checkpoints/048000` on Iridis. Next: Stage 3c (recovery demos) and/or more demos to fix the remaining grasp misses, then Stage 4 (human-data comparison) and Stage 5 (SmolVLA).
